Kinig pod wiring: ESP32 + INMP441 + SSD1306

From firmware/kinig_pod/config.h and hardware/wiring.md (HomemadeCookie/Kinig). Pin order matches a 38-pin ESP32 DevKit.

  • Power both modules from 3V3, not 5V. The INMP441 is a 3.3 V part.
  • L/R to GND puts the mic on the left slot; the firmware reads I2S_STD_SLOT_LEFT, 32-bit, 16 kHz mono.
  • I2C runs at 100 kHz. OLED pin order differs between modules; match by the printed label, not position.
  • No button. Start and Stop are on the hub's /host page. Flash at 115200 baud.
